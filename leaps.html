LEAP calls are call options with a year or more to expiry — a leveraged, defined-risk way to own a strong stock's next 1–2 years. This screen scores every ticker 0–100 on a rising long-term trend, relative strength, fundamentals, low volatility (cheaper premium), liquidity and an unextended entry, then suggests contracts from the live chain by delta: stock replacement (Δ≈0.80), balanced (Δ≈0.65) and aggressive (Δ≈0.50).
